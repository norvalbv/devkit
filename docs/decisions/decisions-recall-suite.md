# decisions-recall-suite

## Target · 2026-07-25 — Recall is measured by a deterministic suite over a FROZEN corpus, scored on five separate denominators

**Context:** Retrieval quality was measured by three unit assertions and nothing else, so a 27% candidate-set hole survived unnoticed until a hand-run probe found it. The obvious fixes are all traps: scoring against the live docs/decisions tree lets labels rot silently (the gate under test WRITES that tree, and an ABSTAIN case stays correct only until someone rules on it); an LLM-judged scorer reintroduces the per-run token cost this whole effort exists to remove; and a single blended headline lets one failure mask another, since better ranking cannot fix an absent candidate and a retriever that answers everything scores well on recall precisely by being wrong about abstention.
**Ruling:** A new suite (gate-engine/decisions/eval/recall/) scores guard-decisions query --json with pure set arithmetic and substring matching — zero LLM calls, seconds per run, therefore affordable per-commit. It reports FIVE independent metric families against five separate denominators and NO headline number: Containment@5, Buried@5 (conditional on containment), CSA/SFER, FANR/FAR as a raw 2x2, and SetRecall + macro PartialRecall. The corpus is always a FROZEN snapshot hashed as storeHash, never the live tree. Two tiers: a committed neutralised seed corpus that reproduces real measured pathologies (both schema generations, a re-target, a Target falsified by its own notes, duplicate live rulings, an INDEX hole, a dead INDEX row) which CI scores; and an uncommitted local snapshot via --corpus for evidence-only numbers. A mechanical token-Jaccard leakage gate fails any question above 0.5 overlap with its gold axis.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: The failure modes are now separately visible instead of inferred. First run on the seed corpus: Containment 3/3 SINGLE and 1/1 MULTI, but SFER 1/1 (the stale audit ruling returned as if live) and FANR 3/3 (every adversarial near-miss answered) — the two known-unfixed modes, quantified, with the fixes they gate sequenced behind them.
- Negative: Deterministic scoring cannot judge an answer's faithfulness, only whether the right axis and block came back — so a retriever returning the correct axis with misleading prose scores clean. Seed-corpus numbers prove the instrument, NOT real-world recall, and must never be quoted as the latter. And the honest gold set is expensive: roughly 19 hours of human labelling, most of it non-delegable because a wrong ABSTAIN or CURRENT_STATE label inverts the signal rather than adding noise.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling. Rule 12 leans on this store being answerable; an unmeasured retriever is how it silently stopped being so.
**Researched:** arXiv sweep (sc-1236): BEIR 2104.08663 for containment-style measurement over a fixed corpus, and its nDCG@10 headline deliberately NOT copied (k exceeds the agent's read budget; graded labels will not be authored). SQuAD 2.0 1806.03822 for adversarial near-miss ABSTAIN construction — random negatives overstate abstention quality. StratRAG 2604.22757 for breaking multi-hop out rather than averaging it away. Doc2Query-- 2301.03266 / InPars-v2 2301.01820 for the mandatory independent re-derivation filter: unfiltered generation is actively harmful, not merely noisy. That filter earned its keep immediately — it rejected 7 of 15 seed cases, including one whose gold label would have penalised a retriever for correctly returning BOTH sides of a contradictory pair.
**Rejected:** (a) Score against the live docs/decisions tree — labels rot the moment the gate writes, and the gate writes constantly; (b) an LLM-judged relevance scorer — reintroduces per-run token cost and run-to-run nondeterminism in what should be a stable tripwire; (c) one blended headline or an F1 over the abstention 2x2 — lets a false-answer rate be traded against a false-abstain rate invisibly, when the two have opposite costs; (d) MAP / nDCG@10 — need graded labels we will not author, and are the metrics most distorted by pooled-judgment incompleteness at this corpus size; (e) commit the real product corpus for fidelity — devkit installs globally and is ruled to ship the generator, never the data.
