# reviewer-claim-measurement

## Target · 2026-09-05 — Reviewer claim validity is separate from blocking and target attribution

**Context:** Retrospective capture of the sc-2493 and sc-2831 choices implemented and investigated on 2026-09-05. The scale benchmark truncated finding text and credited nearby locations; the static repair benchmark credited a failure in the expected lens. PR600 then demonstrated that fixing the reported classifier behavior could leave the intended defect intact. Treating those signals as correct diagnoses would reward unrelated alarms and make repeated false findings disappear inside a reassuring recall number.
**Ruling:** For correctness scale replay and its reporting-family audits, measure complete recorded reviewer claim occurrences before location matching or semantic grouping. Keep execution, capture completeness, factual truth, introduced-change scope, reviewer charter and intended-defect attribution separate. A verdict, expected lens, nearby location, developer waiver or confident model judgment cannot establish claim truth or target diagnosis. Bind assessments to exact claim and evaluated-source identities, retain unresolved and missing-evidence outcomes, and preserve all occurrences after grouping so repeats remain measurable. Blinded factual assessment precedes separately recorded label comparison; changed assessments append rather than rewrite prior evidence. Precision and simulated parent attribution apply the existing human-or-known-cross-family AI eligibility and verified-context constraints; eligible AI assessments still are not human ground truth. Parent attribution requires a complete task roster and sufficient compared-label evidence. These rules govern offline correctness measurement, not automatic production waivers or a replacement for the existing verdict scorer. The completeness suite retains its separate [open-ended gold-slot](open-ended-reviewer-gold-slots.md) matcher and audit contract.
**Consequences:**
- Positive: Maintainers can distinguish blocking a buggy change from finding its intended defect, and measure invalid-alarm burden without deleting repeated occurrences or hiding unresolved judgments. Historical proxy scores remain interpretable alongside stronger evidence.
- Negative: Complete-claim capture and adjudication cost more than location matching. Missing source context, capped historical labels, unknown adjudicator identity and correlated occurrences can leave production attribution or precision unresolved even after every model call completes. No universal independent-sample confidence interval or human-truth claim follows from an AI census.
**Vision-fit:** n/a — internal tooling: review decisions supported by inspectable evidence.
**Researched:** PR596 (capture/adjudication), PR597 (complete replay), PR600 (reporting-family counterfactual); [claim workflow](../benchmarks/claim-adjudication.md), [complete replay](../benchmarks/experiments/2026-09-05-sol-claims/README.md), [reporting audit](../benchmarks/reporting-family-audit-2026-09-05.md). [MalPR-Bench v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25730v1) separates verdict, target identification and evidence; it motivates the distinction, not the truth of devkit correctness labels. This record is retrospective, not a preregistration of those runs.
**Rejected:** Location or lens matching as factual truth — loses because PR600 reproduces a credited off-target claim; score only unique or resolved claims — loses because repeats and uncertainty are the operational burden under investigation; let offline AI assessments rewrite live gate verdicts — loses because exposure, selection and adjudicator error do not establish production blocking authority.
