## Why public scores do not answer our question

[Macroscope's September 2025 benchmark](https://macroscope.com/blog/code-review-benchmark) selected self-contained runtime defects and reported detection over 118 bugs. Its [February 2026 v3 post](https://macroscope.com/blog/code-review-v3) describes a different precision-oriented evaluation. Neither denominator is our repaired-diff acceptance rate, and their percentages must not be combined into one comparison.

[CodeRabbit's March 2026 Martian discussion](https://www.coderabbit.ai/blog/coderabbit-tops-martian-code-review-benchmark) contrasts offline gold matching with online developer-action signals and highlights missing gold issues. That supports separating target hits from valid extras. Developer action is a behavioral proxy, not proof that a reported issue is factually correct; online recall proxies are not exhaustive counts of every latent defect. We have not reproduced these third-party benchmarks or made a head-to-head ranking.

## Falsifiable local hypothesis

The intervention is one additional same-pass step: connect a proposed failure to a reachable post-change path using source and language semantics. Keep model, context availability, lenses and existing verdict policy fixed. The risk is that extra tracing costs time or makes the reviewer miss valid edge cases. Four exposed pairs can reveal regressions; they cannot establish the proposed mechanism's benefit. Resolve direct caller controls and freeze independent confirmation before promotion.
