CONTRADICTIONS

- Cache scope: FLOW says cache stores PASS "per diff hash" and implies replay on identical bytes; TELEMETRY §6 calls any identical-diff rerun "waste" — wrong because FAIL/inconclusive are never cached (verified above: 95 inconclusive + 19 fail of the 233). FLOW is right on mechanism; TELEMETRY's number is wrong.
- Cache key bytes: FLOW = raw `diffText`; ship-gates-converge note 2026-08-07 + reviewers.mts docblock = `diffCacheIdentity` (normalized). PRIOR-ART correctly notes the normalization; FLOW does not.
- Cache-hit rate: TELEMETRY gives 26–48% from `cached` flag and a different count from `commit_cache_events` (749 hits vs 528 cached=1 for correctness across the DB). Two sources, unreconciled; also `review:completeness` has 563 hits in cache_events but no scope rows at all, so "completeness cache-hit rate" is unmeasurable from the scope table.
- Attempt counts: PRIOR-ART 19 branches/max 49 (gate-events.jsonl) vs TELEMETRY 23/max 55 (usage.db) vs devkit-only 0/19 (above). All three are "the" number somewhere.
- Message in cache key: FLOW says completeness keyed on branch+normalized_message; FLOW also says "message OUT of every reviewer cache key" — both true but FLOW's Open Question 1 treats this as unresolved when judge-verdict-cache-scope Rejected (b) already rules that keying on "diff minus failing reviewer's files" is UNSOUND because a fix routinely edits files outside that reviewer's domain — which is exactly proposal (1)'s incremental window.
- Lens split: BENCH/FLOW say the 4-way split is the shipped default; correctness-lens-split-ab-unresolved says "the split stays dark… nothing published". correctness-lens-split-shipped supersedes it — cite the later one, but note the A/B was UNRESOLVED (net +4 flips against a ~5-flip floor), i.e. the only in-repo splitting precedent did not clear its own bar.

STRONGEST-COUNTERARGUMENT (none of the five made it)

The retry loop is dominated by serial single-finding disclosure (one `--fail "reason"` per lens, 93% of blocked ships = 1 reviewer, 93% of correctness FAILs = 1 finding) plus an ~14% model-invariant false-block rate (correctness-reviewer-precision) — neither depends on how many bytes the judge sees. Incremental windowing cannot lower the attempt count below the number of distinct findings the reviewer will disclose one at a time, and is ruled UNSOUND by judge-verdict-cache-scope Rejected (b) (fixes routinely touch files outside the failing reviewer's domain; writer/reader findings are cross-root by charter). Chunked parallel review raises per-attempt findings (≥4 lenses × N chunks) and restores the 14–31% of diffs already truncated at 60KB — both increase blocks — while ship-gates-converge Rejected (a) forbids a "wall of AI findings". Net: the proposal optimizes the cheapest measured component (~$40 verified identical-PASS waste; correctness per-run $0.67–2.36) and risks the expensive one (attempt count), on a problem that for devkit itself (max 19 attempts, review ≈ deterministic blocks) is smaller than framed. The already-ruled, unbuilt K-sample self-consistency (precision) and the deterministic-gate 21% are bigger, decision-aligned levers.

MUST-EXPERIMENT

1. Finding-location audit: for ≥50 correctness fail→fail consecutive pairs (cost era), classify the k+1 finding as (a) in lines changed k→k+1, (b) in unchanged lines already reviewed at k, (c) in OMITTED/truncated segments. Only (b) is addressable by incremental review; (c) is made worse-by-design. No assumption substitutes — the fingerprint is diff-identity-derived and cannot answer this.
2. Chunked vs whole on the existing bench at realistic scale: build a "scale" corpus track (BENCH §GAPS) of multi-file 5–50KB rows with known cross-file (writer-reader) gold and measure blockRecall/cleanPass pooled + per-lens, chunk-merge consistency, and cost/row — pre-registered with the 0.75/0.85 floors; include a haiku-per-chunk arm only as a separate pre-registered arm against correctness-reviewer-precision's sonnet pin.
3. Disclosure-count vs convergence: A/B "report every failing lens finding this run" (≤4/attempt, still inside the lens structure) vs status quo on live ships, measuring attempts-to-green and agent-confusion proxies (re-fail same category, inconclusive rate, checklist-void under concurrency) — this tests ship-gates-converge Rejected (a) directly and decides whether the loop is serial-disclosure-bound before any windowing work.
